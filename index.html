How Loop works

Find what’s nearby

See nearby venues, local activity, and places where people are already gathering, all through Loop’s map and live venue discovery experience.

Read the vibe

Explore venue details, ratings, comments, and local activity so you can get a feel for a place before deciding whether to go.

Pick the place

Turn a venue or location into a simple plan. Choose where to meet, invite people, and make real-world connection feel easier.

Rally the crew

Use group chat to bring people into a plan, agree a time, and keep the conversation focused around where you are going.

Make plans naturally

Chat around places, times, and simple meet-up ideas so conversations can move from browsing to actually making a plan.

Get answers

The in-app Ask Concierge helps users understand onboarding, location, posting, buzz, privacy, safety, and how to get the most from Loop.
