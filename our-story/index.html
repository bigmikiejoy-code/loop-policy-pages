Two brothers.
One idea.
A little less pressure to say hello.

01 / The idea

What if the place came first?

We kept coming back to a simple thought: people don't always need another way to scroll through strangers. Sometimes they just need a reason to walk into a place, feel comfortable and see what happens.

We wanted to make it easier to discover where people were gathering, and to take some of the awkwardness out of starting a conversation.

02 / The brothers

Two different personalities. The same idea.

Christopher was the outgoing one. I was the one more inclined to think things through — probably a bit too much at times. He'd have happily put our first version out into the world while I was still trying to make the map behave.

We didn't always agree on when something was ready. But we agreed on why it mattered: meeting people should feel like an opportunity, not a test.

03 / The early days

A stubborn map and a lot of conversations.

Years before today's AI coding tools, we experimented with an early map and ideas for connecting people who might enjoy being in the same places. It wasn't polished. Sometimes it barely worked. But we had started building something together.

And if Christopher had been in charge of the launch button, we'd probably have released it much sooner.

04 / Carrying it forward

Some ideas are worth coming back to.

Christopher died before we could finish what we'd started. For a while, the project stopped too.

Eventually I picked it up again. Not because the app had to stay exactly as we'd imagined it, but because I wanted to give our idea a real chance to become something useful. He would have wanted me to make something of it — and he'd definitely have had a few things to say about how long I've spent getting it ready.

05 / Today

Now we're inviting people in.

Loop Venues is live on iPhone and Android. We're starting close to home, in Devon, and learning from the people who use it. The app will keep changing as we discover what really helps people find their places and their people.

Wherever it goes from here, I'll always remember where it began.

The idea at the heart of Loop
The app today

Less scrolling. More living.

Loop Venues is a social discovery app for adults. It starts with real venues and gives you ways to discover what is happening locally and connect with people, without turning every outing into a forced introduction.

01

Start somewhere real

Explore nearby pubs, cafés and other venues. Find somewhere that suits your mood rather than spending the evening staring at profiles.

02

Get a feel for the place

Explore venue details and community conversation. Find a natural starting point for talking, making plans or trying somewhere different.

03

Let connection be your choice

When the conditions are right, Loop's proximity experience can quietly suggest that a compatible person is nearby without immediately revealing who. There's no obligation to do anything.

Just getting started? Local communities grow with the people who join them. Every genuine conversation helps.

Starting in Devon

Good places bring people together.

We're building Loop's early communities around places people already love. If you run a pub, bar, café, venue or local group in Tiverton, Exeter or the wider South West, we'd love to explore a simple local trial together.

No grand promises. Just a chance to see whether Loop can help more people discover your place and feel welcome there.
